`neurone_musicale.pdf` — the original essay (in French) describing the
philosophical and musical foundations of the tool:

> **Programmer un neurone musical — Vers une (re)construction deleuzienne de
> la phrase musicale**
> Antoine Goudreau

It develops, among other things:

- the notion of *co-comprovisation* with the machine;
- a critique of the sequential/hierarchical paradigm of computer-assisted
  composition, opposed to the suppleness of pen-and-paper writing;
- memory, forgetting, and error as generators of variation in oral
  traditions, and the Deleuzian concepts (*virtual/actual*,
  *arborescent/rhizomatic*, *deterritorialization/reterritorialization*)
  applied to the human–machine feedback loop — the wasp and the orchid;
- the full description of the transcription and analysis pipeline.

The technical documentation of the pipeline in this repository
([docs/algorithm.fr.md](../docs/algorithm.fr.md)) is an abridged, structured
version of the essay's analysis sections. The LaTeX source is not included;
contact the author for it.
